Matobur is free to download and play with limited content.   • Plans: Matobur Premium is offered as weekly (1 week), monthly (1 month) and yearly (1 year) auto-renewing subscriptions. The price and length of each plan are shown in the app, in your local currency, before you buy. Prices may vary by country.
  • Payment: payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase or, if your plan starts with a free trial, when the trial ends.
  • Automatic renewal: the subscription renews automatically for the same length and at the same price unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends. On Google Play, the subscription renews unless you cancel it before the end of the current period.
  • Free trial: the yearly plan may include a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends (on Google Play: before it ends), the paid yearly subscription starts automatically when the trial ends. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

In the app, Parent Zone → Settings → Manage subscription takes you there. Cancelling stops future renewals, and Premium stays active until the end of the current paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
